# Contributor License Agreement — SheetSmith

> **This is a draft, not a legal document reviewed by a lawyer.** Have it checked
> before you rely on it. It is written in plain language on purpose, so that both
> sides understand what they are agreeing to.

Thank you for wanting to contribute to SheetSmith.

This agreement is between you (the **Contributor**) and the SheetSmith project
(the **Project**). It covers only code, data and documentation you send us — not
anything you wrote before, and not anything you use privately.

## 1. What you keep

You keep the copyright to your contribution. We are not asking you to hand it
over.

## 2. What you give the Project

You grant the Project a licence to your contribution: worldwide, non-exclusive,
royal-free, perpetual and irrevocable, to use, copy, modify, create derivative
works of, publicly display, publicly perform, sublicense and distribute it as
part of SheetSmith, and to sublicense the copyright and patent rights needed for
that.

Two practical consequences:

* the Project may distribute your contribution under the terms in
  [LICENSE](LICENSE) (PolyForm Shield 1.0.0);
* the Project may keep your contribution in the repository and put your name in
  the history, if you want.

## 3. Patents

If the Project uses your contribution to make something you have or later get a
patent for, you grant the Project a licence to that patent. This is the standard
patent clause; it exists so that a contributor cannot block the project with a
lawsuit after handing over code.

## 4. Your contribution is your own work

You confirm the contribution is yours, or that you have the right to give it away
under this agreement. Do not paste in code from a project whose licence forbids
it, and do not paste in anything confidential from an employer. This is the part
contributors most often get wrong, and it is the part that gets a project into
trouble.

## 5. No warranty

The contribution is provided as is, without warranty of any kind. If it breaks
your game, that is between you and you.

## 6. How to agree

Send a pull request. Opening one means you accept these terms — that is the
standard mechanism and it keeps the paper trail in the repository.

If your employer requires a separate process on your side, tell us before you
start and we will sort it out.

## 7. Changes to this file

We may update this agreement. Changes apply only to contributions sent after the
change; anything already merged keeps the terms it was sent under.
